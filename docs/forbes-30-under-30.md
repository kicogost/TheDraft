# Forbes 30 Under 30, working notes

Researched 30 September 2026. A two year goal, recorded so the deadlines do not get missed.

## Eligibility

Born 16 December 2001, so he turns 30 on 16 December 2031. The rule is under 30 by 31 December of the list year, which leaves **roughly six cycles**, not two.

## The mechanics

**Forbes España 30 Under 30.** Annual. Free self application through a form on forbes.es, not a closed nomination. For the 2026 list, applications closed **30 March** and the list published **1 July**. Thirty places across every field: AI, science, business, sustainability, fashion, music, culture, sport, social innovation. Forbes España states it is not a popularity contest and that a single well documented candidacy carries the same weight as many.

**Forbes 30 Under 30 Europe.** Separate list, **300 places across 10 categories**, with Spaniards on it every year. Ten times the places. Apply to both, every eligible year.

**Judging criteria**, across editions: funding, revenue, social impact, scale, inventiveness, potential.

**Next window:** applications roughly January to March 2027, published July 2027.

## The candidacy is RallyUp, not The Draft

Run The Draft against those six criteria and it scores on almost none. No funding, minimal revenue, modest scale, and writing a newsletter is not inventive. A newsletter is a channel, not a story.

RallyUp is the story: equity partner and chief of staff at a VC backed company, joined after a pivot at about $1,000 MRR, revenue thirty seven times higher within four months, closing on $1M ARR, a $138k contract with an $11bn company, and the role itself won at 23 from one cold message.

**The differentiator is the AI operations angle**, and it is already true. He runs the operation of a near $1M agency single handed because he built its internal tooling himself: proposal generation, a voice engine, call scoring, lead capture.

The Draft's role is the public trail. It makes him findable and legible to an editor doing five minutes of research. It supports the application. It is not the application.

## The structural problem

Forbes business categories skew hard toward founders. Chief of staff is an unusual entry, and the numbers cited are the company's, which point at the CEO. Small companies rarely place two people. An agency at $1M ARR also does not read as scale against a field containing eight figure raises.

## Two moves that change the odds

1. **Apply to Europe as well as Spain.** Same evidence, ten times the places.
2. **Tell Alec before applying, and ask him to back it.** The application cites his company's revenue. A founder who knows and supports it is worth far more than one who finds out afterwards, and a supporting statement is exactly the third party validation the process rewards. It also avoids both of them applying blind.

## The plan

Apply for the 2027 list expecting a long shot and treating it as a rehearsal. The application forces the story onto paper and exposes which parts are thin. Apply again in 2028 with independent revenue attached and the AI tooling documented publicly.

## One caution

Search results for this are dominated by PR agencies selling 30 Under 30 strategy packages. **Do not pay any of them.** The application is free and it is the same door. That industry exists because the credential is desirable, not because it is gated.
