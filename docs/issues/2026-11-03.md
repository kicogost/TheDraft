## 01 The play

### One cold DM, 12 months later

Last year I started following Alec Kremins. He posted about corporate life and marketing, and every post felt like it was written for me.

Then I watched him get fired by beehiiv and funded by a well-known VC in under two weeks.

That was the moment. A founder with fresh money, a blank page and nobody to build it with yet.

I sent him one message. He replied in thirty minutes and booked a call for the Wednesday.

What happened next is the part nobody posts about:

- We spent three months building a SaaS together.
- It got to $1,000 MRR and stopped there.
- After a lot of arguing, I said: we're good storytellers, why don't we start an agency?

For a VC-backed founder, that sounded like a step backwards. Two weeks later a Sequoia partner said AI-powered service businesses were the future, and it suddenly didn't sound so crazy.

We pivoted on 1 May. Six months later we're at **$69,632 MRR, closing in on $1M ARR**, building audiences and GTM engines for CEOs and execs at companies that have raised millions.

No new round. Client results are the only number we track.

Here's what was in the message, and what you can steal.

### 1. Follow before you message.

I'd read his posts for months before I wrote a word. By then I knew what he cared about and how he talked. Write to them in their own language.

### 2. Wait for the trigger.

"Fired to funded in under two weeks" told me exactly what he needed next. Open on that moment, not on your name. They should know why you're writing before they know who you are.

### 3. Make the message the proof.

Mine said I'd found him the same way I'd find his future customers. It wasn't a claim about outreach. It was outreach, landing in his inbox while he read it.

### 4. Send it before it's polished.

There's a typo in the best sentence. He replied anyway.

> **Do this week:** pick one founder you'd love to work with. Read their last 20 posts, wait for the trigger, and send one message that proves what you'd do for them.

## 03 Steal this

The hook from my best post of the week by reach: 2,338 impressions, 12 saves, 19 comments.

> "I got a $100k+ startup job without any experience.
>
> (from someone who got really good at making founders reply)"

**✂️ COPY THIS**

1. **I got [outcome] without [the thing everyone assumes you need].**
2. **(from someone who [odd, specific proof you can back up])**
3. "The secret is in mastering [one tactic]."
4. Numbered steps, each with a real example from your own story.
5. End with "comment below and I'll send you [the exact asset]".

The brackets do the work. The outcome makes people stop, the missing credential makes it feel possible for them, and the proof makes them believe you.
