## Keyword variants

Add every variant to the trigger: upper case, title case and lower case (`STORY`, `Story`, `story`). The caption must contain the keyword exactly as you want people to type it.

`DM` is short and appears inside other words and comments ("DM me", "admin"). If ManyChat's match is "contains", it will fire on unrelated comments. Use the exact-match option for it, or swap it for `COLDDM`.
