The growth video list

Marketing and distribution is the most valuable skill you can build in 2026, and these eight free videos teach it better than most paid courses do.

What this is

I'm a partner at RallyUp, where we write LinkedIn for B2B founders and executives. When a founder asks me how to get better at marketing, these are the videos I send. I checked every link and running time on 2 November 2026.

The honest promise. You get good at marketing by doing it, and eight videos won't change that on their own. Watch one, try one thing from it this week, and only then start the next.

Alex Hormozi · 1h 25m

Everything from writing hooks and ads to what he calls the only four ways to get customers: warm outreach, cold outreach, content and paid ads.
