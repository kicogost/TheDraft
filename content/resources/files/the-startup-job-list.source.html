10 job sites · all startup-friendly

The startup job list

Ten places to find startup jobs that beat scrolling LinkedIn, with the link to each and what it's best for.

What this is

I got my startup job without applying anywhere: I sent the founder one cold message. You still need to know which companies are hiring, though, and these are the places I'd look. I checked every link on 2 November 2026.

The honest promise. A job board puts you in the same queue as everyone else. To skip it, find the role here, then message the person who runs the team directly with a small piece of the work already done.

The ten

01

Wellfound

Startup-only

Built for startups, with salary and equity shown up front on most roles. Its Curated programme asks candidates to reply to companies within a week.

Open → https://wellfound.com/jobs

02

Y Combinator's Work at a Startup

YC companies

Every job at a Y Combinator company in one place. If you want to work at a YC startup, start here.

Open → https://www.workatastartup.com/jobs

03

Welcome to the Jungle (formerly Otta)

Curated tech roles

Welcome to the Jungle bought Otta in 2024 and rebranded it. It narrows the list to roles that match you, which saves you scrolling through hundreds.

Open → https://app.welcometothejungle.com

04

Underdog.io

Startups apply to you

You apply once and the companies come to you, with direct intros to founders and hiring managers.

Open → https://underdog.io

05

Built In

Company culture

Shows you what it's like to work somewhere before you apply, with culture, benefits and photos. Some large companies are on there too, so filter by size.

Open → https://builtin.com/jobs

06

startup.jobs

Startup-only

A free job board for tech startup roles, updated daily.

Open → https://startup.jobs

07

Hacker News: Who is hiring?

Monthly thread

On the first weekday of every month, hundreds of companies post jobs in one thread. The link changes every month, so start from this page and open the latest one.

Open → https://news.ycombinator.com/submitted?id=whoishiring

08

VC job boards

Portfolio companies

Big VC firms list every open role across the companies they've invested in. Three of the largest:

General Catalyst → https://jobs.generalcatalyst.com/jobs a16z → https://jobs.a16z.com/jobs Sequoia → https://jobs.sequoiacap.com/jobs

09

We Work Remotely

Remote

Remote jobs only, running since 2011.

Open → https://weworkremotely.com

10

Lenny's Jobs

Vetted roles

The job board from Lenny's Newsletter. Lenny says every company on it is vetted, with ghost jobs and staffing-agency posts filtered out.

Open → https://www.lennysjobs.com

How to use the list

  1. Pick three companies you'd love to work at from these boards.
  2. Find who runs the team and read their last 20 posts.
  3. Message them directly. Open with something they wrote, and send a small piece of the job, done, before they ask for it.
